# Flexible Deployment

Memori Enterprise runs entirely inside your infrastructure. Two deployment models are available, letting you match Memori to your operational requirements, compliance posture, and team capabilities without compromising on data sovereignty.

## Deployment Models

| Model | Operated by | Best for |
| ----- | ----------- | -------- |
| **VPC** | Memori, inside your cloud account | Teams that want managed operations with all traffic inside their network boundary |
| **On-Premises** | Your team, with Memori support | Regulated industries, air-gapped environments, strict residency requirements |

In both models, memory data never leaves the boundary you control. The difference is who handles day-to-day operations.

## VPC

Memori deploys and operates inside your Virtual Private Cloud on AWS, GCP, or Azure. Your team grants the required account permissions; Memori provisions all resources inside your VPC, handles upgrades, and provides SLA-backed support. You own the data and the network path.

Choose VPC when you want the isolation of a private deployment without taking on the operational work. See [VPC](/docs/memori-enterprise/vpc/overview) for architecture details and network requirements.

## On-Premises

Memori installs on your own hardware or private cloud and runs entirely under your operational control. Memori provides installation packages, detailed runbooks, upgrade procedures, and SLA-backed support. Your team owns day-to-day operations.

Choose On-Premises when you have an air-gapped environment, strict data residency requirements, or a compliance requirement that prohibits third-party management of production systems. See [On-Premises](/docs/memori-enterprise/deployment/on-prem) for infrastructure requirements and deployment process.
