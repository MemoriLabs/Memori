# Getting Started with Memori Enterprise

Memori Enterprise is deployed directly into your infrastructure. This page covers what the onboarding process looks like and what to prepare before your first deployment.

## What to Prepare

Before your deployment begins, gather the following:

| Item | Why it matters |
| ---- | -------------- |
| Infrastructure access | Memori needs credentials or a deployment account for your VPC or on-prem environment |
| Agent inventory | A list of the agents and processes that will read from or write to Memori |
| Data residency requirements | Region constraints for memory storage and processing |

## First Steps After Access

Once your deployment is live:

1. **Log in to your Enterprise dashboard**: Your organization owner receives an invite to the Memori Enterprise dashboard.
2. **Create your first Memory Pool**: Define a shared pool for your highest-priority agent workflow. See [Memory Pool](/docs/memori-enterprise/memory-pool/overview).
3. **Connect your agents**: Use the Memori SDK to connect your agents to your deployment.
4. **Review the audit log**: Confirm memory reads and writes are being recorded correctly before expanding to additional agents.
