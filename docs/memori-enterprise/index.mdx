# What is Memori Enterprise?

**Memori Enterprise** is the production-grade deployment of Memori's agent-native memory infrastructure, designed for organizations that require full data sovereignty, regulated workload compliance, and enterprise-scale reliability.

Memori Enterprise runs entirely inside your environment: VPC or on-premises infrastructure.

## Why Memori Enterprise?

### Full Data Sovereignty

Your memory data never leaves your environment. Memori Enterprise deploys directly into your cloud or on-prem infrastructure, so every memory capture, augmentation pipeline, and recall query stays within your security perimeter. This architecture satisfies strict data residency, compliance, and audit requirements without compromise.

### Dedicated Infrastructure

Enterprise deployments run on isolated infrastructure: no shared compute, no shared storage, no shared network paths. You get guaranteed resource allocation, predictable performance at scale, and the operational isolation that regulated workloads demand.

### Enterprise-Grade Compliance

Memori Enterprise is built for regulated industries. SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance controls are built into the architecture, not bolted on. Immutable audit logs, role and relationship-based access control (ReBAC), and integrated SSO give your security and compliance teams the visibility and control they require.

### Custom SLA and Solutions Engineering

Every Memori Enterprise deployment includes a custom SLA and a dedicated solutions engineering team. From architecture review and deployment planning through production go-live and ongoing optimization, Memori's solutions engineers work alongside your team.

## Deployment Options

Memori Enterprise supports two deployment models:

| Deployment | Description |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| **VPC** | Memori deployed inside your Virtual Private Cloud. Memori handles deployment, upgrades, and operations. All traffic stays inside your network boundary. |
| **On-Premises** | Memori deployed and operated entirely on your own infrastructure. Full operational control with Memori-supported runbooks, upgrade procedures, and SLA-backed support. |

Both models support private connectivity to MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and other supported databases within your infrastructure boundary.

## Enterprise Features

All Memori Enterprise deployments include:

- **Unlimited memories**: No per-memory or per-recall pricing. Every production plan includes unlimited memories created and recalled.
- **Memory pools**: Shared, permissioned collections of memories available to agents across users, teams, workflows, and applications.
- **Agent Access Control**: Role and relationship-based access control (ReBAC). Assign owner, admin, and user roles, or map directly to your existing policies.
- **SSO and OAuth**: Integrate your identity provider to centrally manage permissions. Supports SAML, OIDC, and OAuth 2.0.
- **Full observability**: Memory telemetry exported into your existing observability stack. Immutable audit logs showing who accessed what, when, and why.
- **Advanced evals**: Evaluation and holdout attribution to continuously measure and compound agent performance gains in your environment.
- **Outcome-based pricing**: Available for qualifying deployments. Pricing tied to measurable agent performance outcomes.

## Performance

Memori Enterprise uses the same Advanced Augmentation pipeline as Memori Cloud. On the LoCoMo long-conversation memory benchmark, Memori achieves **87% accuracy** while using an average of **721 tokens per query** (just **2.8% of full-context footprint**), delivering over **36× cost savings** compared to full-context prompting.

Enterprises on Memori production plans see up to **97.2% token-cost reduction** on peer-reviewed benchmarks, with agents that perform better across every session because they carry structured memory forward.
