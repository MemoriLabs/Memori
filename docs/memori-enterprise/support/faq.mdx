# FAQ

### What is Memori Enterprise?

Memori Enterprise is a production-grade deployment of Memori's agent-native memory infrastructure that runs entirely inside your environment — your cloud account, VPC, or on-premises hardware. Your memory data never leaves the boundary you control.

### How is Enterprise different from Memori Cloud?

**Memori Cloud** is a fully managed, multi-tenant service. You connect your agents with an API key and Memori handles everything. Data is stored in Memori's infrastructure.

**Memori Enterprise** runs inside your infrastructure. You own the data, the network path, and the storage. Memori handles deployment and operations (for BYOC) or supports your operations team (for on-premises). Enterprise also includes dedicated infrastructure, custom SLAs, SSO, advanced access controls, compliance documentation, and solutions engineering.

### Which deployment model is right for us?

**Choose BYOC** if you want managed operations but require data to stay in your own cloud account or VPC. Memori deploys and operates inside your environment; you own the data.

**Choose On-Premises** if you have strict data residency requirements, an air-gapped environment, or a compliance requirement that prohibits third-party management of production systems.

See [Deployment Overview](/docs/memori-enterprise/deployment/overview) for a full comparison.

### What databases does Memori Enterprise support?

MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MariaDB, including managed providers (AWS RDS, Aurora, Atlas). Additional backends are available on request. Your solutions engineer will confirm support for your database during scoping.

### Is Memori Enterprise SOC 2 Type II certified?

Yes. Memori Labs maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance covering Security, Availability, and Confidentiality. The report is available under NDA. Contact [enterprise@memorilabs.ai](mailto:enterprise@memorilabs.ai).

### Is a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) available for HIPAA?

Yes. A BAA is available for healthcare organizations. Contact [enterprise@memorilabs.ai](mailto:enterprise@memorilabs.ai) before deployment.

### Can Memori Enterprise run in an air-gapped environment?

Yes, with the on-premises deployment model. The only external communication required is optional telemetry (fully opt-out) and support channels (configurable). Your solutions engineer will document exact network requirements during scoping.

### How is pricing structured?

Enterprise pricing is based on your deployment model, scale, and contract terms. Outcome-based pricing (tied to measurable agent performance improvements) is available for qualifying deployments. Contact [Memori Enterprise Sales](https://memorilabs.ai/contact) for a pricing conversation.

### How long does deployment take?

For BYOC, typical deployment takes one business day after prerequisites are complete. For on-premises, timelines depend on your infrastructure setup; your solutions engineer will provide an estimate during scoping.

### What support is included?

All Enterprise plans include SLA-backed support with defined response windows, a dedicated solutions engineering team, upgrade coordination, and runbooks. Specific SLA terms are defined in your contract.

### How do I get started?

Contact [Memori Enterprise Sales](https://memorilabs.ai/contact). A solutions engineer will reach out to schedule a scoping call.
