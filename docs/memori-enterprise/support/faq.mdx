# FAQ

### What is Memori Enterprise?

Memori Enterprise is a production-grade deployment of Memori's agent-native memory infrastructure that runs entirely inside your environment: VPC or on-premises infrastructure. Your memory data never leaves the boundary you control.

### How is Enterprise different from Memori Cloud?

**Memori Cloud** is a fully managed, multi-tenant service. You connect your agents with an API key and Memori handles everything. Data is stored in Memori's infrastructure.

**Memori Enterprise** runs inside your infrastructure. You own the data, the network path, and the storage. Memori handles deployment and operations (for VPC) or supports your operations team (for on-premises). Enterprise also includes dedicated infrastructure, custom SLAs, SSO, advanced access controls, compliance documentation, and solutions engineering.

### Which deployment model is right for us?

**Choose VPC** if you want managed operations but require data to stay in your own cloud account. Memori deploys and operates inside your VPC; you own the data.

**Choose On-Premises** if you have strict data residency requirements, an air-gapped environment, or a compliance requirement that prohibits third-party management of production systems.



### Can Memori Enterprise run in an air-gapped environment?

Yes, with the on-premises deployment model. The only external communication required is optional telemetry (fully opt-out) and support channels (configurable). Your solutions engineer will document exact network requirements during scoping.

### How is pricing structured?

Enterprise pricing is based on your deployment model, scale, and contract terms. Outcome-based pricing (tied to measurable agent performance improvements) is available for qualifying deployments.

### How long does deployment take?

For VPC, typical deployment takes one business day after prerequisites are complete. For on-premises, timelines depend on your infrastructure setup; your solutions engineer will provide an estimate during scoping.

### What support is included?

All Enterprise plans include SLA-backed support with defined response windows, a dedicated solutions engineering team, upgrade coordination, and runbooks. Specific SLA terms are defined in your contract.
