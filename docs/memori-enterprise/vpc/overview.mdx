# VPC

Memori Enterprise deploys inside your Virtual Private Cloud on AWS, GCP, or Azure. Every component (memory capture, Advanced Augmentation, Intelligent Recall, and the enterprise control plane) runs within your network boundary. No traffic leaves your VPC.

## What runs inside your VPC

```
Your VPC
├── Memori Enterprise (compute)
│   ├── Capture service
│   ├── Augmentation pipeline
│   ├── Recall service
│   └── Enterprise control plane (ReBAC, SSO, audit)
└── Your storage (private subnets)
    └── Audit log storage
```

All traffic stays within your VPC. No public endpoints are required.

## Private connectivity

Memori uses the private connectivity options native to your cloud provider:

| Cloud | Private connectivity options |
| ----- | ---------------------------- |
| **AWS** | VPC endpoints, PrivateLink, VPC peering, Transit Gateway |
| **GCP** | Private Service Connect, VPC peering, internal load balancers |
| **Azure** | Private Endpoints, VNet peering, Private Link |

## Network requirements

We will provide a complete network requirements checklist during scoping. At a high level:

- **Inbound:** Your application layer reaches Memori's capture and recall endpoints over private subnets. No public IP required.
- **Outbound:** All outbound traffic stays within your private subnets. No public exposure required.
- **Egress:** Optional telemetry export and SLA-covered support channels. Fully configurable; air-gapped operation is available for on-premises deployments.

## Security groups and firewall rules

Memori's deployment automation generates a minimal set of security group rules during provisioning. Rules follow the principle of least privilege:

- Memori compute accepts traffic only from your application subnets on the Memori API port
- Memori compute reaches only your designated internal endpoints
- No inbound rules are opened to the public internet

Your security team reviews all rules before deployment goes live.

## Deployment

VPC deployment is handled by Memori's deployment automation inside your account. Your team grants the required IAM permissions; Memori provisions all resources within your VPC and hands you a private endpoint for your agents to connect to.
