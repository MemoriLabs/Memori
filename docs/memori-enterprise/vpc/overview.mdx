# VPC

Memori Enterprise deploys inside your Virtual Private Cloud on AWS, GCP, or Azure. Every component, memory capture, Advanced Augmentation, Intelligent Recall, and the enterprise control plane, runs within your network boundary. No traffic leaves your VPC.

## What runs inside your VPC

All Memori services run on compute inside your VPC, connected to storage on your private subnets. No public endpoints are required. The services include the capture service, augmentation pipeline, recall service, and enterprise control plane (ReBAC, SSO, audit).

## Private connectivity

Memori uses the private connectivity options native to your cloud provider:

- **AWS**: VPC endpoints, PrivateLink, VPC peering, Transit Gateway
- **GCP**: Private Service Connect, VPC peering, internal load balancers
- **Azure**: Private Endpoints, VNet peering, Private Link

## Network requirements

At a high level:

- **Inbound**: your application layer reaches Memori's capture and recall endpoints over private subnets. No public IP required.
- **Outbound**: all outbound traffic stays within your private subnets. No public exposure required.
- **Egress**: optional telemetry export and SLA-covered support channels. Fully configurable; air-gapped operation is available.

We provide a complete network requirements checklist during scoping.

## Security groups and firewall rules

Memori's deployment automation generates a minimal set of security group rules during provisioning. Rules follow the principle of least privilege: Memori compute accepts traffic only from your application subnets on the Memori API port, reaches only your designated internal endpoints, and opens no inbound rules to the public internet. Your security team reviews all rules before deployment goes live.

## Deploying to your VPC

### 1. Start a scoping conversation

Email [support@memorilabs.ai](mailto:support@memorilabs.ai) to begin. We review your architecture, confirm region and connectivity requirements, and document the IAM permissions needed.

### 2. Grant account permissions

Your team grants the required IAM permissions to Memori's deployment account. Memori does not retain standing access; permissions are scoped to the deployment operation.

### 3. Deployment

Memori's deployment automation provisions all resources inside your VPC and hands you a private endpoint for your agents to connect to.

### 4. Validate

End-to-end validation runs against your environment with Memori engineers on the call.

<Admonition type="tip">
  After validation, configure the Memori harness plugin with your private endpoint URL. Run a short harness session and confirm memories appear in Memory Explorer.
</Admonition>
