# On-Premises

On-premises deployment gives you complete operational control. Memori runs on your own hardware or private cloud, operated by your team with Memori-supported runbooks, upgrade procedures, and SLA-backed support. No external network access is required.

## When to choose on-premises

- Regulated industries with strict data residency or sovereignty requirements
- Air-gapped environments where outbound internet access is restricted or prohibited
- Organizations that require full operational control over all infrastructure
- Compliance requirements that prohibit third-party management of production systems

## Architecture

Memori installs on your infrastructure and runs entirely within your private network. The only external communication is optional telemetry (fully opt-out) and SLA-covered support channels.

```
Your Infrastructure
├── Application layer (your agents)
├── Memori Enterprise (installed and operated by your team)
│   ├── Memory capture and augmentation
│   ├── Intelligent Recall
│   └── Enterprise controls (ReBAC, SSO, audit)
└── Your storage
    └── All memory data stored here
```

## Infrastructure requirements

We will provide exact sizing guidance based on your workload. General requirements:

| Component | Minimum | Notes |
| --------- | ------- | ----- |
| Compute | 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM | Per Memori node; scales horizontally |
| Storage | 50 GB | For Memori operational data; memory data is stored in your infrastructure |
| Network | Private network access | No public internet required |
| OS | Linux (Ubuntu 22.04+, RHEL 8+) | Container runtime required |

## What Memori provides

- Installation packages and deployment automation scripts
- Detailed runbooks for installation, upgrades, scaling, and incident response
- Configuration templates for your identity provider and infrastructure
- SLA-backed support with defined escalation paths
- Scheduled upgrade coordination: Memori tests releases before you apply them

## Deployment process

1. **Architecture review**: We review your environment, confirm sizing, and document connectivity requirements.
2. **Installation package**: Memori provides versioned installation packages and runbooks.
3. **Installation**: Your team installs Memori following the provided runbooks. Memori is available throughout.
4. **Validation**: End-to-end validation against your environment, with Memori engineers on the call.
5. **Handoff**: Your team owns ongoing operations; Memori provides support and upgrade coordination under your SLA.
