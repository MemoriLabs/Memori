# On-Premises

On-premises deployment gives you complete operational control. Memori runs on your own hardware or private cloud, operated by your team with Memori-supported runbooks, upgrade procedures, and SLA-backed support. No external network access is required.

## What it's for

On-premises is the right choice when your environment is air-gapped, when compliance requirements prohibit third-party management of production systems, or when you need full operational control over every layer of the stack.

## When to choose on-premises

- Regulated industries with strict data residency or sovereignty requirements
- Air-gapped environments where outbound internet access is restricted or prohibited
- Organizations that require full operational control over all infrastructure
- Compliance requirements that prohibit third-party management of production systems

## Infrastructure requirements

General requirements, we provide exact sizing guidance based on your workload:

- **Compute**: 4 vCPU, 16 GB RAM per Memori node; scales horizontally
- **Storage**: 50 GB for Memori operational data; memory data is stored in your infrastructure
- **Network**: private network access required; no public internet required
- **OS**: Linux (Ubuntu 22.04+, RHEL 8+) with container runtime

## What Memori provides

- Installation packages and deployment automation scripts
- Detailed runbooks for installation, upgrades, scaling, and incident response
- Configuration templates for your identity provider and infrastructure
- SLA-backed support with defined escalation paths
- Scheduled upgrade coordination: Memori tests releases before you apply them

## Deployment process

### 1. Architecture review

We review your environment, confirm sizing, and document connectivity requirements. Reach out to [support@memorilabs.ai](mailto:support@memorilabs.ai) to begin.

### 2. Receive the installation package

Memori provides versioned installation packages and runbooks tailored to your environment.

### 3. Install

Your team installs Memori following the provided runbooks. Memori engineers are available throughout the installation.

### 4. Validate

End-to-end validation against your environment with Memori engineers on the call.

<Admonition type="tip">
  After validation, run a short harness session and confirm memories appear in Memory Explorer before rolling out to your full team. Your SLA and upgrade coordination begin from this point.
</Admonition>
