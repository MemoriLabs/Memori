# Deployment Overview

Memori Enterprise runs entirely inside your environment. No memory data, augmentation output, or recall queries leave the boundary you control. Memori handles deployment, upgrades, and operations under your SLA; you retain full data sovereignty.

## Deployment Models

| Model | Who operates Memori | Where data lives | Best for |
| ----- | ------------------- | ---------------- | -------- |
| **VPC** | Memori (inside your VPC) | Your cloud account or VPC | Teams that want managed operations with all traffic inside their network boundary |
| **On-Premises** | Your team, with Memori support | Your own hardware or private cloud | Regulated industries, air-gapped environments, strict residency requirements |

Both models connect privately to the databases you already run — MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and others — inside your infrastructure boundary.

## What Memori Manages

Regardless of deployment model, Memori provides:

- Deployment automation and runbooks
- Upgrade procedures and release management
- SLA-backed support with defined response windows
- Solutions engineering during onboarding and beyond

## Database Connectivity

Memori Enterprise connects to your existing databases over private network paths. No public endpoints are required and no data transits the public internet.

Supported storage backends:

- **MongoDB** — Full support including Atlas and self-managed replica sets. TTL indexes for retention, replica sets for durability.
- **PostgreSQL** — Including AWS RDS, Aurora, Neon, Supabase, and self-managed instances.
- **MySQL / MariaDB** — Including AWS RDS and Aurora.
- **Additional backends** — Available on request. Contact your solutions engineer.
