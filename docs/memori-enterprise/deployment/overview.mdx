# Deployment overview

Memori Enterprise runs entirely inside your environment. No memory data, augmentation output, or recall queries leave the boundary you control. Memori handles deployment, upgrades, and operations under your SLA; you retain full data sovereignty.

## Deployment models

<Properties>
  <Property name="VPC">
    Operated by Memori inside your VPC. Data lives in your cloud account or VPC. Best for teams that want managed operations with all traffic inside their network boundary.
  </Property>
  <Property name="On-premises">
    Operated by your team with Memori support. Data lives on your own hardware or private cloud. Best for regulated industries, air-gapped environments, and strict residency requirements.
  </Property>
</Properties>

## What Memori manages

Regardless of deployment model, Memori provides:

- Deployment automation and runbooks
- Upgrade procedures and release management
- SLA-backed support with defined response windows
- Dedicated support during onboarding and beyond
