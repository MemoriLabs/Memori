# Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC)

BYOC deploys Memori entirely inside your cloud account or VPC. Memori's team handles deployment, upgrades, and day-to-day operations, while your data stays within the boundary you control. You get managed operations without a second vendor holding your memory data.

## How It Works

Memori is deployed into a dedicated environment within your cloud account. All compute, storage connections, and network paths are provisioned inside your VPC. Your data never transits to Memori's infrastructure.

```
Your VPC
├── Application layer (your agents)
├── Memori Enterprise (deployed by Memori, inside your VPC)
│   ├── Memory capture and augmentation
│   ├── Intelligent Recall
│   └── Enterprise controls (RBAC, SSO, audit)
└── Your database (MongoDB, PostgreSQL, etc.)
    └── All memory data stored here
```

## What You Provide

- A cloud account (AWS, GCP, or Azure) with sufficient quota for Memori's resource requirements
- VPC with private network access to your database
- IAM roles or equivalent permissions for Memori's deployment automation
- Your solutions engineer will provide a detailed requirements checklist during onboarding

## What Memori Provides

- Full deployment automation — Memori provisions and configures everything inside your account
- Upgrade management — Memori coordinates all version upgrades with you, tested before rollout
- Monitoring and alerting — Memori monitors availability and performance under your SLA
- Runbooks — Documented operational procedures shared with your team
- On-call support — SLA-backed incident response

## Supported Cloud Providers

- Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
- Microsoft Azure

Private cloud and sovereign cloud deployments are available on request.

## Deployment Process

1. **Scoping call** — Your solutions engineer reviews your architecture, confirms cloud provider and region, and identifies your database endpoints.
2. **Requirements checklist** — Memori provides a preflight checklist covering IAM requirements, network configuration, and quota.
3. **Deployment** — Memori provisions the environment inside your account. Typical deployment takes one business day after prerequisites are complete.
4. **Validation** — Memori and your team run end-to-end validation against your environment.
5. **Go-live** — Your agents connect to the Memori endpoint inside your VPC.
